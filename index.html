The Experience

Look for the Blue Gate on the corner.

Tucked away in the historic Chugureti district on Dimitri Uznadze Street, Ninia's Garden unfolds behind an understated blue door into one of Tbilisi's most enchanting dining destinations.

Whether you are gathering around our courtyard fire pit under twinkling garden lights or relaxing in our opulent indoor hall with warm wooden tones and glowing fireplace warmth, Ninia's offers an unforgettable backdrop for contemporary Georgian cuisine.
